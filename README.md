# TeckPac LC Consulting Website

A modern, responsive website for TeckPac LC — a technical consulting firm specializing in food and beverage packaging solutions.

This site showcases TeckPac LC’s expertise in closures, bottles, materials, and packaging systems, along with real-world case studies, training offerings, and consulting services.

---

## 🚀 Overview

TeckPac LC provides hands-on technical consulting across the full packaging lifecycle — from concept to commercialization to troubleshooting in-market failures.

This website is designed to:

- Clearly communicate service offerings
- Highlight real-world problem solving
- Provide credibility through case studies and training
- Convert visitors into leads via contact and inquiry forms

---

  - Bottles (ISBM, EBM, PET, HDPE, PP)

## 📊 Featured Case Studies

### Cracked Beverage Closures

- Identified widespread closure cracking across multiple product lines
- Investigated molding parameters, torque, resin, and design stress points
- Isolated a single dominant change driving failure
- Delivered a targeted fix that resolved returns quickly

### Carbonation Loss in Soft Bottles

- Customer complaints of taste issues and bottle deformation
- Root cause traced to preform defects causing seal inconsistencies
- Implemented corrective action and inspection improvements
- Restored product integrity and customer satisfaction

### Rapid Product Commercialization

- Took a beverage concept to market in **5 months**
- Leveraged existing packaging assets to reduce capital expenditure
- Delivered speed-to-market without sacrificing quality
